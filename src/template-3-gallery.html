Our Story

Raised in the
Coulee Region

Steve still farms the same land his father and grandfather did: land that raised his children and is now cherished by his grandchildren. A member of the American Wagyu Association, he has built a herd of full blooded Wagyu cattle at least 98% pure, every animal's genetics verified by the American Wagyu Association. We are dedicated to raising the finest quality Wagyu with humane practices and in a sustainable way, as stewards of the land that has been in this family for generations.

Steve & Sally MillerOwners
